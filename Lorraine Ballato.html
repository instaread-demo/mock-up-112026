We are coming into the home stretch and just about finished with hydrangeas for this season. Except there’s one more task that needs to be dealt with: hydrangeas and deer control. I wish this was a “one and done” chore, but unfortunately you’ll have to repeat it at least once, if not more often.

Growth of the Deer Population

Over the course of the 36 years I have lived at my current address in Connecticut, deer pressure has gone from zero to a continual concern. The herd multiplies every year, and depending on the severity of the climate, it’s possible that winter kill will not reduce that population. Their only enemy is automobiles. If you live in “deer country” like I do, you must decide how you want to handle this challenge.

Deer Repellent Products

I have trialed many products, and although most of them work, some work better than others. The one that has been consistently effective for me is Deer Defeat.

Deer Defeat deer repellent

One spring I trialed it on a container of tulips—a deer favorite. There were 2 other products in the trial (no names mentioned). The plants I sprayed with Deer Defeat were untouched for the entire trial period of 10 weeks, while the other two were consistently nibbled. Deer Defeat has great staying power and doesn’t easily wear off/wash off. You should also know that it is the stinkiest of all the products I have EVER tried. So be prepared to immediately toss the clothes you wear when you have finished applying it into the laundry.

The better news is that the owners of Deer Defeat are offering my subscribers free shipping. Just use the code AUTUMN27, which will provide free shipping until December 31st. Grab it now and start spraying, especially your old wood plants (big leaf, mountain, and oak leaf hydrangeas). Of course it also works well on the rest of your garden. We had a situation in our neighborhood a few years back when deer actually stepped onto someone’s front porch to eat the holiday greens on the wreath. Incredible!
