Composer Julian Revie joins host David Ball in this episode of Sounds from the Sanctuary to introduce Illumina – an immersive, multimedia project being premiered during the Jubilee Year of St. Francis at Christ Cathedral. The project brings together choral music, handbells, the Hazel Wright Organ, a lightshow, and birdsongs collected from over 30 countries to create an immersive experience of beauty and faith in the sacred architecture of Christ Cathedral. Find out more at christcathedralmusic.org and spiritus.art
