Is Paris closed in August? Which Paris restaurants are open in August 2026? Where can you eat in Paris during the summer holidays? These are the questions we get every year, and this guide answers all of them — with confirmed closing dates for more than 120 of the best restaurants in Paris, sorted by neighborhood and by week.

The short answer: no, Paris does not close in August. It’s one of the most persistent myths about visiting Paris in summer. Yes, many Paris restaurants close for summer holidays (the fermeture estivale or fermeture annuelle you’ll see taped to doors), but those closures are concentrated in a narrow window — roughly August 3 through August 23. Even at the quietest point of the month, dozens of our favorite Paris restaurants stay open and are happy to feed you. Before August 3 and after August 24, the city runs close to normal.

Which Restaurants Are Closed for Summer (and Which Aren’t)

So where should you eat in Paris in August? We wrote to every restaurant we recommend and heard back from more than 120 of them with their confirmed August 2026 summer schedules. Below you’ll find exactly which restaurants are open and which are closed, organized two ways:

  • Summer closing dates by neighborhood (arrondissement) — every restaurant, from the 1st to the 20th, with its exact closing dates. If you know where you’re staying, start here.
  • What’s open week by week — the three weeks in the heart of August (August 3–9, 10–16, and 17–23) when a table is hardest to find, with every restaurant open at least four days that week.

This covers the full range of where to eat in Paris in August: bistros, brasseries, wine bars, Michelin-starred tables, neighborhood favorites, and destination restaurants across every arrondissement. Whether you’re looking for a classic Paris bistro that stays open all summer, a restaurant near the Louvre or the Eiffel Tower, or somewhere open on a specific August date, this is your guide.

Click any restaurant’s name for our full review — address, what to order, opening hours, and all the practical details. We update this post throughout the summer as more restaurants confirm their plans.
