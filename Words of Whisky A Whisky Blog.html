The Quiet Rebels series from Nc’nean has arrived at Matthew, the distillery’s blender. The series is meant to highlight team spirit, but also emphasizes the diversity of whiskies coming out of Nc’nean.

Matthew Hastings, nicknamed “bunny” (check his signature on the box), spent quite a few years bartending in London. During that time a love of dry sherry was sparked. His entry into the Quiet Rebels series celebrates that style of sherry.

An important driver of this whisky’s flavour are the Manzanilla hogsheads – they make up 47% percent of the cask recipe, complemented by 51% bourbon casks and 2% Oloroso sherry butts. The Manzanilla casks were sourced from José y Miguel Martín in Jerez and seasoned for a minimum of 24 months.

The spirit itself was made from 100% organic Scottish barley, distilled between September 2017 and April 2020.

Nc’nean Quiet Rebels Matthew (48.5%, OB, 2026)

Nose: A mixture of ripe green grapes, salty butter and limestone with a toasty, nutty edge as well. There’s a hint of fresh pineapple too, as well as preserved lemons. A touch of chamomile.
Taste: Tad austere with a stronger nuttiness, almost to the point of spiciness, which is trademark Nc’nean for me. Very gentle yellow fruit notes, a sliver of caramel and some butter too. Maybe a pinch of salt alongside crushed mint leaves, but also stewed fruits.
Finish: Medium length. Lingering quinces, honey and light mineral notes.

Conclusion
One that improves with each sip, often revealing something new, although I’m sure there’s a point of diminishing returns. For now though, I'll gladly keep on sipping.
8.6
