Sweet & Spicy Candied Beef Bacon with Jalapeño (Easy 5-Ingredient Recipe)

If you love the combination of sweet, smoky, and spicy, this Sweet & Spicy Candied Beef Bacon needs to be on your must-make list!

Thick, savory slices (I used Godshall’s Beef Bacon) get a delicious flavor upgrade with coconut sugar, cracked black pepper, fresh jalapeño, and a drizzle of real maple syrup. As the bacon bakes, the sweet coating caramelizes, the jalapeños bring a little heat, and everything comes together in one seriously crave-worthy bite.

It’s a fun twist on traditional candied bacon and makes an easy appetizer for game day, holiday gatherings, weekend brunch, or anytime you’re craving something a little different.

Easy Ingredients for Sweet & Spicy Candied Beef Bacon

You don’t need a long grocery list or a complicated sauce to make this recipe – there are only FIVE easy ingredients.  The combination is simple, but the flavor is anything but boring. Sweet, smoky, savory, and spicy all at once

  • Beef Bacon: The star of the show! Naturally wood-smoked beef bacon delivers a rich, savory flavor that pairs beautifully with the sweet and spicy toppings.
  • Coconut Sugar: Adds sweetness and helps create that delicious caramelized coating. If preferred, you can swap in brown sugar.
  • Coarse Ground Black Pepper: Brings a little bite and balances the sweetness.
  • Fresh Jalapeño: Thin slices add a pop of color and just the right amount of spicy heat.
  • Real Maple Syrup: A drizzle adds natural sweetness and helps bring all the flavors together.

Tips for Making the Best Candied Beef Bacon

  • Use a wire rack: Elevating the bacon on a rack over a foil-lined baking sheet allows heat to circulate and helps the bacon cook more evenly.
  • Cover baking sheet in foil: The bacon isn’t cooking directly on the foil (I don’t like that), but it IS catching the sugar and fat drippings and making clean-up so much easier.
  • Slice the jalapeños thinly: This distributes the pepper flavor and heat without overpowering the beef bacon.  You can omit if you don’t like them, and/or just seed them to take the heat level down some.
  • Keep an eye on the oven: The coconut sugar and maple syrup can caramelize quickly. Start checking the bacon around the 20-minute mark to prevent the sugars from burning.
  • Let it cool slightly: The sweet coating will continue to set as the bacon cools, making the slices easier to handle and serve.

What Is Beef Bacon?

Now, let’s talk about the star ingredient! If you’ve never tried beef bacon, you might be wondering what it actually is. Is it just regular bacon made from beef? What cut of beef does it come from? And does it taste like traditional pork bacon?

Beef bacon is made from beef rather than pork, but the process is similar to traditional bacon. The meat is cured, seasoned, and smoked to develop that familiar savory, smoky flavor. Beef bacon is cut from the beef plate (aka short plate) a primal cut found in the lower belly of the cow, situated just below the rib section and next to the brisket.  Then it’s typically wood-smoked for a rich, hearty flavor. It’s a delicious alternative when you want to switch up your usual pork bacon routine.

What Does Beef Bacon Taste Like?

Beef bacon has its own personality! While pork bacon has a familiar salty, smoky flavor, beef bacon offers a heartier, more beef-forward taste. It’s savory, rich, and deliciously smoky, especially when cooked until the edges become crisp. It’s a fun way to bring something different to the table, whether you’re making breakfast, building a sandwich, or adding a little something extra to an appetizer – or just don’t do pork in your diet.

Is Beef Bacon High in Protein?

Beef bacon provides protein along with plenty of savory flavor. A 1 ounce serving has about 4 grams of protein per serving and zero carbohydrates per serving. Be sure to check the package of the brand you’re using for the exact serving size and the full nutrition information.

 Ways to Use Beef Bacon

  • Crumble over baked potatoes or sweet potatoes.
  • Add to burgers and grilled sandwiches.
  • Chop into salads or protein bowls.
  • Use as a topping for deviled eggs.
  • Wrap around appetizers.
  • Add to breakfast tacos or scrambled eggs.

Sweet & Spicy Candied Beef Bacon with Jalapeño

Sweet, smoky, spicy, and ridiculously easy! An easyoven-baked appetizer for game day, brunch, holiday entertaining, or anytimeyou're craving something different.

Servings: 4 slices
Ingredients
  • 4 oz 4 slices @Godshalls Beef Bacon
  • ¼ cup coconut sugar
  • 1 tsp coarse ground black pepper
  • 1 jalapeño sliced very thinly
  • 4 tsp real maple syrup
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil and top with a wire rack.
  3. Arrange bacon in a single layer. Sprinkle evenly with coconut sugar and black pepper, gently patting into the bacon.
  4. Arrange jalapeño slices over the bacon and drizzle maple syrup down the center of each slice.
  5. Bake for approximately 25–28 minutes, or until crisping. Keep an eye on it after 20 minutes so the sugars don’t burn.
